### Key Engineering Highlights:
- **VaultFlex™ 5-in-1 Multi-Position Hinge System**: Precision die-cast CNC-machined hinge locks at 5 configurations — symmetrical A-frame stepladder, lean-to extension ladder, 90° staircase mode, twin-tower scaffold base, and a 270° single-side fold for ultra-compact storage in an Indian apartment or car boot — replacing five separate ladders with one versatile unit.
- **Extra-Wide 260 mm Non-Slip Platform Steps**: Ribbed groove surface with moulded heel-lock lip provides 260 mm of standing depth per step — 40% wider than standard Indian stepladders — offering fatigue-free standing during long overhead painting, wiring, and ceiling repair tasks in Indian homes.
- **Integrated Tool Caddy & Pail Shelf**: Moulded polypropylene tray mounted at the top rest provides a 5 kg rated tool station for paint brushes, rollers, spirit levels, drills, and mobile phone — eliminating constant up-and-down trips that cause 63% of ladder falls on Indian residential sites.
