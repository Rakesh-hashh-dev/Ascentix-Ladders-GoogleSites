### Story Copy:
Every year, the National Crime Records Bureau (NCRB) and India's National Safety Council report tens of thousands of serious injuries from ladder and elevated-work falls — making height-related accidents one of the leading causes of occupational fatality across India's booming construction and electrical sectors. For generations, the fundamental design of the Indian commercial ladder remained unchanged: thin-gauge aluminium extrusions, brittle plastic rivets, and flexing stiles that invite instability under heavy tool loads.

**Ascentix Ladders Pvt. Ltd.** was incorporated with a singular, uncompromising objective: **To treat elevation equipment with the rigorous engineering standards of aerospace structures — and make that standard accessible to every Indian tradesperson and contractor.**

Founded by veteran mechanical and materials engineers with experience in India's aerospace and defence sectors, Ascentix re-engineered climbing hardware from the molecular level up. We replaced cheap alloys with 7075-T6 aircraft aluminium, incorporated genuine Toray 3K woven carbon fibre, and invented multi-stage air-damping mechanisms that eliminate pinch hazards — delivering BIS IS 4130 / IS 4131 certified safety to electricians, civil contractors, telecom tower crews, and facility managers across India.
