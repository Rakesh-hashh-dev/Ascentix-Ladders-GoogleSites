### Key Engineering Highlights:
- **RoofGrip™ Stainless Steel Ridge Hook System**: Grade 304 stainless steel dual-arm ridge hook rated to 3× operator body weight (450 kg proof load) locks over any rooftop parapet wall or ridge tile — enabling hands-free descent on rooftops up to 65° pitch without a separate safety rope, eliminating the #1 cause of fatal falls among Indian solar EPC installers.
- **Integrated Pitch Angle Clinometer**: A precision bubble clinometer gauge bonded directly to the rail reads the current roof pitch in real-time from 0°–75°, with colour-coded safe/caution/danger zones (green 15°–60°, amber 60°–65°, red above 65°) — ensuring solar technicians confirm safety before the first step up.
- **UV-Stabilised Marine-Grade 6082-T6 Aluminium**: ASTM B117 salt-spray tested for 1,000 hours, UV-resistant clear anodising rated for 10+ year outdoor exposure in India's coastal Maharashtra, Goa, Tamil Nadu, and Kerala climates — where conventional ladders oxidise and pit within two monsoon seasons.
