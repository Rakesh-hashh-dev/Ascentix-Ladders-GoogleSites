### Key Engineering Highlights:
- **4-Level Tool-Free Height Adjustment (1.0m / 1.5m / 2.0m / 3.0m)**: Single-operator height change in under 60 seconds using spring-loaded stainless steel pin-lock column joints — no spanners, no ladders, no second person required. Each height level is rated for the full 200 kg platform capacity, enabling one DeckRise to replace fixed-height platforms at multiple racking levels in Indian e-commerce fulfilment centres and retail planogram reset operations.
- **Pneumatic Auto-Leveling Feet (±35mm Floor Compensation)**: Four independent pneumatically-actuated leveling feet automatically compensate for uneven concrete warehouse floors with up to ±35mm variance per leg — the most common issue in Indian warehouse construction where floor flatness tolerances are wider than international norms. No manual cranking, no bubble levels, no productivity lost to setup.
- **Expandable Deck Panel System (800mm → 1,200mm)**: The base 800 × 600mm aluminium platform deck expands laterally to 1,200mm with a tool-free clip-on extension panel — enabling two-person simultaneous work on a single platform, the only Indian rolling work platform with lateral deck expansion for planogram builds, HVAC ductwork, and overhead conveyor maintenance.
