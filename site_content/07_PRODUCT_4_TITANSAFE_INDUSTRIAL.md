### Key Engineering Highlights:
- **360° Enclosed Safety Guardrail**: Full-perimeter waist-high safety cage with self-closing spring-loaded gate lets operators work hands-free in any direction without an external harness tie-off — mandatory for Indian factory safety compliance under the Factories Act, 1948.
- **Lockable Auto-Braking Casters**: Spring-loaded 100mm polyurethane wheels roll smoothly across warehouse floors and automatically lock onto heavy rubber feet when stepped on — ideal for large Indian FMCG, automotive, and logistics warehouses.
- **Non-Conductive Pultruded Fibreglass Stiles**: Continuous filament fibreglass moulded under 200 tonnes of hydraulic pressure, dielectric tested up to 35 kV — BIS IS 4130 compliant for Indian high-voltage industrial facilities.
