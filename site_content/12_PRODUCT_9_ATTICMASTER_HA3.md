### Key Engineering Highlights:
- **Gas-Strut Counter-Balance Mechanism (2kg Pull Force)**: A dual-arm nitrogen gas strut system identical to automotive bonnet mechanisms provides perfectly balanced fold-down operation — the 14.5 kg ladder descends smoothly and silently with a single finger pull on the integrated pull-cord handle, requiring only 2 kg of force. No sudden drops, no noise — essential for Indian apartment living where neighbours share ceiling slabs.
- **40mm PIR Foam Insulated Ceiling Hatch Panel**: The hatch panel integrates a 40mm polyisocyanurate (PIR) foam core with reflective aluminium foil facing — providing 30-minute fire resistance rating and an insulation value of R-2.5, significantly reducing AC heat loss through the ceiling opening in Indian hot-climate apartments (Mumbai, Chennai, Hyderabad) where ceiling heat ingress is the primary AC efficiency loss.
- **Variable-Height Telescoping Lower Legs (2.40m–3.20m)**: Spring-loaded telescoping leg sections with 50mm snap-lock increments accommodate India's most common residential ceiling heights from old 8-foot (2.43m) Mumbai apartments to new 10.5-foot (3.20m) premium flat construction — without cutting or trimming the ladder, making installation a clean half-day job for any carpenter.
