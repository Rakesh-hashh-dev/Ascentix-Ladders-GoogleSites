### Key Engineering Highlights:
- **One-Touch Slide-and-Lock Rung System**: Each rung deploys and locks independently with a single hand using the proprietary AeroLock™ positive-engagement button — enabling precise height-by-rung adjustment from 1.0 m to 6.2 m without tools, eliminating the rope-and-pulley fumbling of conventional Indian construction site extension ladders.
- **Aerospace-Grade 6061-T6 Aluminium Alloy Rails**: Cold-drawn, heat-treated, and shot-peened extruded aluminium rails tested to 3× the rated 150 kg load — providing structural rigidity comparable to steel at 60% of the weight, ideal for Indian rooftop, facade, and telecommunications tower work.
- **All-Terrain Self-Adjusting Stabiliser Feet**: Spring-loaded 180° pivoting rubber feet with integrated level indicator automatically conform to slopes up to 15° — essential for India''s varied terrain from rural building sites to urban scaffolding decks.
