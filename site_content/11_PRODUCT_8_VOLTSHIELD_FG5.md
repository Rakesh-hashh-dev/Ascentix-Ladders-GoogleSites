- **Pultruded E-Glass Fibreglass Stiles — 25kV Dielectric Rated**: Continuous-filament E-glass fibreglass stiles manufactured under 200 tonnes of hydraulic pressure achieve a non-conductive dielectric rating of 25kV — exceeding Indian LT/HV distribution safety requirements for 415V and 11kV panel work — at a price accessible to every licensed Indian electrician, not just DISCOM fleet buyers.
- **Dual Safety Red Locking Feet — Visual Open Confirmation**: Colour-coded positive-locking outrigger feet turn from grey (folded) to bright safety red (fully locked open) upon full ladder deployment — eliminating India's single most common step ladder misuse: climbing a partially-opened ladder, responsible for 38% of all step ladder falls at Indian electrical worksites.
- **550mm Wide-Stance Chrome-Moly Spreader Bar**: A 30% wider spreader bar footprint than standard Indian fibreglass step ladders provides rock-solid stability on the uneven mosaic tile, red oxide, and rough concrete floors common to Indian residential and commercial electrical installations.
