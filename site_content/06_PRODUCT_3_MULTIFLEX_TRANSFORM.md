### Key Engineering Highlights:
- **Patented Quad-Lock 360° Hinges**: Hardened stainless steel dual locking pins automatically lock at 0°, 30°, 90°, and 180° angles. Tested to withstand over 1,800 kg shear load — far exceeding BIS IS 4130 hinge integrity requirements.
- **Auto-Leveling All-Terrain Outriggers**: Independent micro-adjustable legs extend up to 250 mm on either side for wobble-free stability on sloping ground, Indian stairwells, uneven rooftops, and construction site ramps.
- **Converts into 14 Configurations in Seconds**: Stepladder (A-frame), uneven staircase easel, straight extension ladder (up to 5.4m), 90° flush wall ladder, and low-profile scaffold base — one tool that replaces four on Indian job sites.
