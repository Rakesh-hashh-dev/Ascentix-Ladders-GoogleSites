---

### Thumbnail Brief

- **Headline text**: *"BIS IS 4130 — Kya Hota Hai?"* (bold, blue on white background)

---

### Full Script

---

**[HOOK — 0:00 to 0:40]**

**Amit**:
> "Bhai log — aapne kitni baar suna hai 'BIS certified ladder'? Packaging par likha hota hai. Dealer bolta hai. Company ka ad bolti hai. Lekin kya aap jaante ho iska actually matlab kya hai? Kya test hote hain? Kaunsa ladder pass karta hai aur kaunsa nahi? Aaj main explain karunga — BIS IS 4130 standard kya hai, kya test hote hain, aur aap ek ladder ko kaise verify karo ki woh genuinely certified hai ya sirf label lagaya hua hai."

---

**[WHAT IS BIS? — 0:40 to 3:00]**

**Amit**:
> "BIS matlab Bureau of Indian Standards — yeh India ka official national standards body hai. Government of India ke under aata hai. Jaise hum electrical wiring ke liye IS 732 follow karte hain — waise hi ladders ke liye IS 4130 aur IS 4131 standards hain."

*[Camera: Amit shows a printed copy of the BIS IS 4130 standard document.]*

**Amit**:
> "IS 4130 — yeh hai portable ladders ka standard. Isme cover hoti hain metal ladders — aluminum, steel. IS 4131 — yeh hai wooden aur fiberglass ladders. Agar ladder manufacturer bolte hain 'BIS IS 4130 certified' — iska matlab unka ladder in specific tests se pass hua hai jo BIS specify karta hai."

---

**[WHAT TESTS DOES BIS IS 4130 REQUIRE? — 3:00 to 7:30]**

**Amit**:
> "Ab dekhte hain — kaunse actual tests hote hain? Main simplify karke explain karta hoon."

*[Camera: Amit on whiteboard/chalkboard with simple diagram, or text overlay graphics.]*

**Amit**:
> "**Test 1 — Load Test**: Ladder pe static load apply kiya jata hai — typically 4 guna rated load. Matlab agar ladder 150 kg rated hai — toh test 600 kg tak hota hai. Ladder ko deflect ya fail nahi karna chahiye."

> "**Test 2 — Rung Strength Test**: Individual rungs par load diya jata hai. Rung bend ya break nahi karna chahiye under specified load."

> "**Test 3 — Stile Integrity**: Stile — yeh side rails hain ladder ke — inki bending strength test hoti hai."

> "**Test 4 — Hardware Test**: Hinges, locks, extension mechanisms — sab individually tested hote hain under repeated use cycles."

> "**For Dielectric Ladders — Test 5: Electrical Insulation**: Fiberglass ya carbon fiber dielectric ladders ke liye — voltage application test hota hai. Ladder ke through leakage current measure kiya jata hai. IS 4130 specify karta hai maximum permissible leakage current jo practically zero hona chahiye."

*[Camera: Amit shows the Ascentix NABL lab test footage on a laptop screen.]*

**Amit**:
> "Yahan dekho — yeh Ascentix ka CarbonApex X1 hai NABL lab mein. 35,000 volts apply kiya gaya. Current reading — 0.00 milliampere. Yahi hota hai genuinely certified dielectric ladder. Test fabricate nahi hota — NABL accreditation ensure karta hai ki test genuine conditions mein hua."

---

**[HOW TO VERIFY A LADDER IS GENUINELY BIS CERTIFIED — 7:30 to 11:00]**

**Amit**:
> "Ab sabse important part — aap verify kaise karo ki ladder genuinely BIS certified hai?"

*[Camera: Amit demonstrates step-by-step on laptop.]*

**Amit**:
> "Step 1 — BIS Care portal check karo. BIS.gov.in par jaao. 'Certified Products' section mein jao. Manufacturer ka naam search karo. Agar genuinely certified hain — wahan listed hongay with their licence number."

> "Step 2 — Certificate physically check karo. Genuine BIS certificate mein hota hai — BIS licence number, product category, date of issue, date of expiry. Agar dealer sirf 'BIS certified' bol raha hai bina document diye — toh caution raho."

> "Step 3 — NABL lab report maango. Dielectric ladders ke liye — NABL accredited test report maango. Yeh public document hai — manufacturer share kar sakta hai."

*[Camera: Shows Ascentix's certificate on camera.]*

**Amit**:
