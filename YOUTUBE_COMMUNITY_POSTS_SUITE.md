### Quiz Post 2: The Fiberglass Inspection Protocol
* **Post Format**: Educational Checklist Image  
* **Text / Caption**:
  > ⚡ *ELECTRICIAN ADVISORY: The Danger of "Fiber Bloom"*  
  > 
  > If you own a fiberglass ladder that is more than 2 years old and frequently exposed to the Indian sun, do this test before tomorrow's shift:  
  > 
  > 1️⃣ Put on a latex glove.  
  > 2️⃣ Rub your palm firmly along the exterior yellow/orange stile.  
  > 3️⃣ If the glove comes back with fine prickly glass fibers or yellow dust—STOP.  
  > 
  > This is **Fiber Bloom** (UV resin breakdown). It allows atmospheric humidity and conductive dust to penetrate inside the stiles, drastically lowering your electrical protection.  
  > 
  > **Fix**: Wash with mild soap, let dry completely, and coat with polyurethane acrylic clear coat, or upgrade to our non-porous resin *VoltShield FG5*.  
  > 
  > Stay safe out there! Save this post for your crew. 📌
