# About Ascentix Ladders

Ascentix Ladders brings together ladder and work-platform design concepts for different access needs. The catalogue helps visitors compare configurations for home improvement, maintenance, workplace and specialist applications.

Our approach is to describe each design clearly, distinguish intended applications and identify specifications that still need confirmation. A product illustration or design proposal is not independent evidence of a tested safety rating.
